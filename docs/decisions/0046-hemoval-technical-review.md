review by the coding agent; it is not clinician approval, source permission, or

## Review findings

The coherence draft correctly keeps WFNS and mRS as separate ordinal values,
preserves missingness, applies the seed-101 reservation before eligibility,
keeps all observed combinations reviewable, and avoids inferred timing,
causality, treatment effects, and death labels for missing mRS. The blind-review
draft correctly requires identical presentation, source/model blinding,
transient mappings, aggregate-only ratings, explicit insufficient-information
responses, and exclusion of the inspected reserved reference.

The two-field task cannot support a complete whole-patient realism claim. The
drafts therefore remain limited to a development review of a recorded
association and missingness handling. No clinical severity threshold,
relationship, or hard plausibility rule is inferred from repository evidence.
The manifest records these boundaries and the current candidate status.

## Decision

Accept the drafts for technical consistency and fictional workflow preparation.
Do not run a patient-level presentation, select a model, or call the result
clinical validation until source permissions and the intended review setting
are established. Do not use any inspected HeMoVal, EARLYDRAIN, or Suwa data as
an untouched final reference. A genuinely independent reference remains the
blocking dependency for the final evaluation step.
